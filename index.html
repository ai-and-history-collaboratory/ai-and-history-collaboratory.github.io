The AI + History Collaboratory

An online forum for historians exploring how artificial intelligence can enhance historical research — from handwritten text recognition to skills, workflows, database interfaces and beyond.

📅 Monthly sessions, 4 pm UK time 🌎 US, UK & Europe 📚 Season 1: Dec 2025 – May 2026

What we do

The collaboratory brings together historians and digital humanists to work on AI-enabled research methods collectively.

Synchronous sessions

Monthly online meetings with presentations, live demonstrations, and structured discussion. Each session focuses on a specific theme chosen by Collaboratory members.

Asynchronous collaboration

Between sessions, members share work via the GitHub repository wiki, discussion board and email — developing tools, testing workflows, and refining research questions.

Three core strands

LLM-enabled handwritten text recognition; development of skills and workflows for historical research; conceptualisation and integration of Model Context Protocol servers into historical research workflows.

Featured Project

A major collaborative initiative emerging from the collaboratory's work.

Opening the Ottoman Archive

A collaboration with Ottoman Turkish scholars to develop two-stage processing of printed and handwritten manuscripts: Visual Capture followed by Semantic Processing. We are building Anthropic-compliant skill files to support both stages for Ottoman Turkish, with plans to extend coverage to Albanian, Bulgarian, Greek, and Armenian.
